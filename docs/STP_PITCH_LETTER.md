# Note to Emmy, Becky, and Pam

*A draft email / cover note you can send with the product overview. Edit freely — this is meant to sound like you, not like a sales brochure.*

---

Hi Emmy, Becky, and Pam —

I've been quietly building something for STP over the last few weeks, and I think it's far enough along that I'd love your eyes on it.

It's a web app I'm calling **Theater App**. The short version: instead of the script living in one Google Doc, the prop list in a spreadsheet, blocking in someone's notebook, and "who has which mic when" living mostly in people's heads, this tries to keep all of that attached to the same living copy of the show.

You import the script, it becomes a timeline of moments (the beats of the show), and then directors can layer casting, props, cues, entrances, exits, blocking, and notes right onto those moments. Actors get a Rehearse mode that actually matches how people practice — full scene, just my lines, or line cues — including an option to blur your own lines until you tap them. Reports (prop sheets, cue sheets, etc.) fall out of that same timeline instead of being maintained separately.

I built it because I keep watching us do great work with tools that weren't really made for theater prep. I'm not trying to replace how STP creates shows — I'm trying to give the production side one place that stays in sync with the words on the page.

A few things I want to be clear about up front:

- **This is an invitation, not a sales pitch.** If it doesn't feel useful, no hard feelings — I'll keep tinkering on my own.
- **If you do see potential, I'm offering to keep building and supporting it for free.** STP would be the first real home for this. My ask isn't money; it's partnership and honest feedback.
- **I want to test it on a real production** — not a toy demo forever. The only way to know if it helps directors, admins, and actors is to run it through an actual STP show and see what breaks, what's missing, and what's genuinely helpful.
- **It's an MVP.** The core prep and rehearsal loops work. Scheduling, attendance, fancy PDF call sheets, and live show-calling are still ahead. I'd rather be honest about that than oversell it.

I've attached a short overview that explains what it does today, who it's for, and what a pilot might look like. If you're open to it, I'd love to do a short walkthrough (15–20 minutes) and then talk about whether there's a show coming up where we could try it for real.

Thanks for reading — and for everything you already do to make STP work.

— Connor
