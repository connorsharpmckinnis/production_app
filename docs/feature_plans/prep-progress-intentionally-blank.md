# Feature plan — Prep progress (“intentionally blank”)

**Status:** Roadmap (curiosity / far-field; approved as eventual candidate)  
**Created:** 2026-07-29  
**Related:** [PREP_READINESS.md](../PREP_READINESS.md), Overview readiness (Phase 8), [PROJECT.md](../PROJECT.md) preparation progress wish

---

## Goal

Move beyond raw counts (“0 props tagged”) to **intentional prep state**: scenes/domains marked reviewed, intentionally empty, or needs work — so leadership trusts Overview during a pilot.

---

## Problem

Readiness scores can punish shows that honestly have no props in a scene, or that haven’t started Act 2 yet. Directors need a way to say “we looked; it’s blank on purpose.”

---

## Proposed direction

- Per scene (or per domain: props/costumes/E/E): `unchecked` | `in_progress` | `reviewed` | `intentionally_blank`
- Overview respects marks when scoring / displaying soft dimensions
- Does not auto-infer “blank” from zero counts without human confirm

---

## Non-goals

- Heavy project-management % complete theater for its own sake
- Blocking production use until 100% reviewed

---

## Recommendation

Schedule after Overview has been used in a real pilot; design marks with whoever lives in Overview weekly.
