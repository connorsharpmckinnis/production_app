# Feature plan — Understudies & cast overrides

**Status:** Roadmap (curiosity / far-field; approved as eventual candidate)  
**Created:** 2026-07-29  
**Related:** [PROJECT.md](../PROJECT.md) understudy mention, casting model, [character-nightly-packs.md](character-nightly-packs.md), [crew-roles.md](crew-roles.md)

---

## Goal

Support **understudies** and temporary **cast overrides** so packs, Rehearse, and call sheets can target the person actually playing tonight without destroying primary casting.

---

## Proposed direction

- Primary cast remains canonical.
- Override: character X → user Y for date range or rehearsal label.
- Packs/call sheets/Rehearse read effective cast.
- Understudy designation without override = planning only.

---

## Non-goals

- Full HR absence management
- Automatic understudy notification blasts (unless email feature exists)

---

## Recommendation

Design after actor prep packs + call sheets, so effective-cast has clear consumers.
