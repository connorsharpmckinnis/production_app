## Scene Eight \- We’re So Back (And Other Such Nonsense)

Note: Hebrews 10:23

Note: “Let us hold unswervingly to the hope we profess, for he who promised is faithful.”

Note: Psalm 116:8-12

Note: For you, Lord, have delivered me from death, my eyes from tears, my feet from stumbling, that I may walk before the Lord in the land of the living. I trusted in the Lord when I said, “I am greatly afflicted”; in my alarm I said, “Everyone is a liar.” What shall I return to the Lord for all his goodness to me?

*LIGHTS UP on SHACKLETON, WILD, and CREAN struggling through the South Georgia mountains and glaciers. They are exhausted, starving, and on the brink of collapse. Some sort of heartbeat-patterned music (Into the Deep with heartbeat drums) plays underneath the dialog*

WORSLEY: Sir, we really should stop and rest. We’ve been hiking nonstop for over 24 hours.

SHACKLETON: You’re right, Worsley. What’s left to eat?

*Everyone checks their pockets for a meager inventory.*

SHACKLETON: Two biscuits, a bar of chocolate, and some seal blubber. Alright, let’s finish it all and get some rest.

WORSLEY: But what if –

SHACKLETON: There’s no use rationing anymore. Either we make it to the whaling station in the next (rubs his belly thoughtfully) eighteen hours, or we don’t.

CREAN: Finally\! (Busts open the chocolate bar eagerly)

WORSLEY: (yawning) Would it be alright if I got a bit of shuteye, sir? It’s awfully cold…

SHACKLETON: Alright. I’ll keep watch.

WORSLEY: (Already falling asleep) Okay, just for half an hour.

*WORSLEY and CREAN cuddle or lean against each other and are asleep almost instantly. SHACKLETON struggles to stay awake on his own, eventually shaking himself and pulling out his journal to occupy himself.*

SHACKLETON: (journaling, exhausted) Not much to report. We’ve run out of food, so we either reach safety in the next day or so, or… (starts to nod off) If I fall asleep, it is likely none of us will ever wake up again.

*The heartbeat music begins to slow and fade, while the lights come down slowly. Everything starts to get quieter, calmer, and more menacing. SHACKLETON hunches in on himself to stay warm, and begins to nod off once more.*

*On the wind, we hear an infinitely faint ‘Ernest’ that repeats itself and builds louder each time. Suddenly, it nearly shouts, booming through the air like a thunderclap.*

*ERNEST\!*

*SHACKLETON jerks awake, looking around for the person who woke him up. Seeing no one, he realized what happened and how close they all were to drifting off to freeze to death. He shakes the others awake[^17]*

CREAN: (groggily) Has it been thirty minutes already?

SHACKLETON: Sure. We need to get moving again.

*WORSLEY and CREAN lurch offstage into the blinding snow. SHACKLETON gets up and looks around, still in a sort of wonder and confusion mixed with concern.*
